Welcome to Wimborne Wayfarers HC

A Little About Us

We are a dynamic, sociable club based at QE Leisure Centre in Wimborne, Dorset.

We combine successful hockey with a friendly, sociable atmosphere, and are always on the lookout for new players, beginner or experienced, to join our Club.

Many of our players have been with us since their Junior Days, and are now a vibrant part of our Ladies & Mens Teams.

If you are interested, dust off that kit, grab a stick and contact us to find your next session.

Our players range from 5 years to 50+ — so whatever age you are, it’s never too late to join us!

Juniors • Safety • Welfare

Junior Hockey & Child Welfare

A warm welcome to everyone returning to hockey and to everyone joining Wimborne hockey for the first time!

Tuesdays • 7–9 yrs (6:00–7:00pm) & 10–13 yrs (7:00–8:00pm)

Safety, Attendance Registration & What to Wear

Safety & Registration: The safety of your children is very important to us. With such a great turn out on a Tuesday night for the under 10’s, please could parents ensure that you register your child’s/children's attendance with Mike when you arrive. If you need to leave and return later, you can allocate responsibility to another parent or provide us with a contact number. When training is finished, please register children out with Mike.

Clothing & Equipment: Please ensure children wear several layers that they can remove as they warm up, plus hats and gloves when cold. Although injury in this age group is minimal, we strongly advise children wear mouth guards and shin pads during training. We always have plenty of spare hockey sticks for children to borrow!
